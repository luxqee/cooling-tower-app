# CT Field Ops — Technician Quick Guide

## Install the App on Your Phone

**iPhone (Safari):**
1. Open Safari and go to the app URL
2. Tap the **Share** button (box with arrow at the bottom of the screen)
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

**Android (Chrome):**
1. Open Chrome and go to the app URL
2. Tap the three-dot menu (top right)
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**

When you first open the app, tap **Allow** when asked about notifications — this is how you find out when your variation is approved.

---

## Clocking In

1. Open the app → tap **Time Tracking** in the menu
2. Select your job from the dropdown
3. Tap **Clock In**
4. The timer starts — leave the app open or come back to it later

## Clocking Out

1. Open the app → tap **Time Tracking**
2. Tap **Clock Out**
3. Done — your hours are saved

> **Important:** Clock in and out every day, even if you're continuing the same job.

---

## Logging a Variation

Use this when you find extra work on site that wasn't in the original scope.

1. Tap **Log Variation** in the menu
2. Select the job
3. Describe what you found (be specific — what part, what problem, what you did)
4. Enter the estimated cost in dollars
5. Optionally, tap the photo field to take a photo as evidence
6. Tap **Submit Variation**

The director is notified straight away. You'll get a notification on your phone when they decide.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| App won't load | Check your signal. Try 4G instead of WiFi |
| Job not in the dropdown | Contact your service manager — you may not be assigned yet |
| Photo won't upload | Try a smaller photo or check your signal |
| Didn't get a notification | Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in your phone Settings |

If something else looks wrong — screenshot it and send to your manager.
