# CT Field Ops — Director Quick Guide

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows:
- **Live Crew** — who is currently clocked in, at which job, and for how long (refreshes every 5 seconds)
- **Hours vs Quoted** — hours logged per job vs the quoted amount

Jobs shown in **red** are more than 10% over their quoted hours — they need attention.

---

## Approving Variations

When a technician submits a variation, you receive a push notification on your phone.

1. Open the app → tap **Variations**
2. Review the description, cost estimate, and photo (if attached)
3. Tap one of: **Approved** / **Rejected** / **Queried**
4. If **rejecting** or **querying**: enter a reason in the text box (required)
5. Tap **Confirm**

The technician is notified of your decision immediately.

**What happens when you approve:**
- The variation amount is added to the job's invoice record automatically
- The technician receives a push notification

**Queried** means you need more information — the technician is notified to clarify.

---

## Managing the Team

1. Go to **Team** in the sidebar
2. Tap **Edit** next to any team member
3. Change their **Role** or set them as **Inactive** if they've left
4. Tap **Save**

> You cannot edit your own account from this page.

---

## Managing Jobs

Go to **Jobs** to:
- Create new jobs (tap **New job**)
- Edit job details — pencil icon on any job card
- Change a job's status (scheduled → active → complete → cancelled)
- Delete a job (trash icon — requires confirmation)

---

## Scheduling

Go to **Schedule** to:
- View the next 14 days of technician assignments
- Assign a technician to a job on a specific date (tap **Assign technician**)
- Remove an assignment (trash icon on any assignment row)

---

## Notes

- Rejected variations must include a reason (minimum 10 characters)
- Approved variations go directly into the invoice record for that job
- Only directors can approve/reject variations and manage team roles
